# Maintainers

This document defines current ownership and maintainer expectations.

## Active Maintainers

- Lead Maintainer (BDFL): `@x24102`

## Ownership Areas

- Product direction and roadmap: Lead Maintainer
- Architecture and trust boundaries: Lead Maintainer
- Contributor process and policy enforcement: Lead Maintainer
- Security response coordination: Lead Maintainer

As the project grows, maintainers may be added with explicit ownership scopes.

## Review Expectations

- First maintainer response target for PRs: 3 business days.
- High-risk changes (security/trust/content integrity): priority review.
- Maintainers enforce policy consistency across docs and code.

## Succession Policy

If Lead Maintainer is unavailable long-term:

1. Existing maintainers nominate interim lead.
2. Governance update PR records transition.
3. Ownership and CODEOWNERS are updated in the same change set.
